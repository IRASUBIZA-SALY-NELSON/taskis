Taskis Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Taskis is a private team chat and task app (website and Android app). Accounts are created by an administrator. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and your choices.

Data we collect

  • Account information: name, username, email address, profile photo and password (stored only as a salted hash).
  • Content you create: chat messages, files, photos and videos you upload or share to Taskis.
  • Calls: audio and video during voice and video calls. Calls are transmitted in real time and are not recorded by us.
  • Device and network data: push notification tokens and endpoints, and IP address (used for security and sign-in protection).

How we use it

To provide the app: sign you in, deliver messages, files and calls, send notifications, and protect accounts against abuse. We do not show ads, do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Sharing

We do not share your data with third parties for their own purposes. We use service providers that process data on our behalf: hosting, a database, file storage, and push notification delivery (such as Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and browser push services). Other members of your Taskis workspace can see the messages, files and profile details you share with them.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Uploaded files are stored encrypted.

Permissions on Android

  • Camera and microphone: for video and voice calls.
  • Photos and files: to attach files and use "Share to Taskis".
  • Notifications: to alert you to new messages and calls.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and data at any time; see the account deletion page.

Children

Taskis is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
